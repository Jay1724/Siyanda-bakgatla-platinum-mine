ABOUT US

Our roots

Siyanda Bakgatla Platinum Mine (SBPM) is a rapidly growing, non-listed black-owned emerging Platinum Group Metal mining and processing business.

[ Site photo — plant / headgear ]

Our operating asset is located within the Limpopo and North West Provinces of South Africa, and our head office is based in the rapidly expanding business hub of Rosebank, Johannesburg. Our business model is to extract and process Platinum Group Metals (PGMs), the transitional metal Chromium, base metals, and other by-products from our reserve base.

MISSION

Creating shareholder value and supporting the economy and development of the region.

VENTURES

Our sister company, Masa Chrome, extracts chrome from our tailings — continuously improving throughput and efficiency through technological enhancement.
