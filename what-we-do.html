WHAT WE DO

Our business and how we create value

We strive to secure, develop and operate a portfolio of high-quality resource assets from which we deliver leading shareholder returns — investing in mining growth and infrastructure to ensure the success and lifespan of our mine.

MINING ACTIVITY

Three-quarters of SBPM's underground mining activities are mined conventionally, using stoping with strike pillars. Opencast mining operations occur on the farm Turfbult.

Underground mining occurs at depths of 850m to 1,400m, while opencast excavations occur in strips up to 100 metres wide and 35 metres deep.

90% of production is focused at the UG2 reef, with other areas of focus at the Merensky reef and the Turfbult farm.

WHERE WE OPERATE

UG2 and Merensky ore, from shaft to concentrate

We currently extract UG2 and Merensky ore from our reserve base via underground shafts and opencast activities. This ore is treated in milling and flotation circuits to produce a final PGM-rich concentrate.
