SOCIAL LABOUR PLAN

Creating value

SBPM has a specific Social Labour Plan (SLP) and Mining Charter community socio-economic development objectives. We create value by investing in health and educational infrastructure in our community.

This investment is in line with the Integrated Development Plans (IDPs) of Moses Kotane and Thabazimbi Local Municipalities, as well as the Bakgatla Ba Kgafela Tribal Authority Masterplan, addressing critical areas of socio-economic need.

Social and Labour Plan projects focus on infrastructure development, education and skills development, health, poverty alleviation, and enterprise development — creating job opportunities and skills development for local communities.
