Leading the future of sustainable mining

With over 40 years of experience, Siyanda Bakgatla Platinum Mine (Pty) Ltd — the Union Mine — is a PGM producing mine acquired from Anglo American Platinum in February 2018 by Siyanda Resources and the Bakgatla-Ba-Kgafela Joint Venture.
