SAFETY

Keeping our workplace safe

At SBPM, we encourage all our employees to prioritise safety, to always wear PPE, and to encourage each other to be safe.

[ Safety signage / PPE photo ]

After significant challenges, the major focus was to return from the National Lockdown. Despite experiencing a tragic fatality in July 2020, we managed to reduce the identified safety risks.

The good news is that our LTIFR injuries are decreasing on a year-to-year basis. This is due to continued focus on compliance to standards — specifically the handling of equipment material, as well as slip and fall injuries contributing to agency areas.
