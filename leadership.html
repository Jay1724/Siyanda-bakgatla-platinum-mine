LEADERSHIP

Driven by principle

Our authorisation and decision framework are made within the ambit of good governance, as prescribed by the principles of King IV — linked to good governance desired outcomes.

Ethical Culture

Leadership and employees uphold the highest values and ethics of the company at all times, conducting themselves with integrity, fairness, respect and honesty.

Good Performance

We focus on effective growth and job retention strategies. After extensive stakeholder engagement, the Board adopted — and continues to review — a four-focus-area strategy.

Effective Control

The Board exercises effective control through its sub-committees: the Audit and Risk Committee, the Safety and Sustainable Development Committee, and the Remuneration Committee.

Legitimacy and Trust

Building meaningful, trusting relationships is foundational to SBPM's leadership. We work continually to improve the quality of our relationships with all stakeholders.
